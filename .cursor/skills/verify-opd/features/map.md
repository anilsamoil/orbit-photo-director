# Map

The Map tab is the globe, the ISS track and marker, target pins, a dropped pin, the legend, the imagery date, the time controls, and the right-hand tool rail.

## Sub-features

- `map-globe` shows the MapLibre canvas, the ISS marker, and the ground track.
- `map-horizon-inset` is the button `[data-pip="horizon"]`, labeled `Show the ISS view`. It is a 148 by 96 preview of the earth from the ISS. The viewport must be at least 800px wide and 600px tall, so it is on iPad Pro 11 portrait and landscape, at 1194 by 710 and 1194 by 700, and on desktop at 1400 by 900, 1280 by 800, and 1280 by 700. It is absent on iPhone 13 and iPhone 17 Pro, in portrait and at 874 by 402, 844 by 390, and 932 by 430. It sits `right: 112px` and `bottom: calc(var(--map-command-bottom, 0px) + var(--map-command-height) + 36px)`, above the time strip and left of the dock. `#map-pane.map-chrome-hidden .pip-horizon` hides it, and so do an open inspector and an open satellite picker. `mountHorizonInset` in `frontend/src/map/index.ts` calls `createIssRenderer` with `{ labels: false }`. One hook slot keeps that preview unmounted while `[data-iss-scene]` exists. Inside the same 800 by 600 query, an open `.view-map .map-legend` has `max-width: calc(100% - 272px)`. Enter or Space on the focused button fires its click. `openTab` in `frontend/src/insets/host.ts` focuses `#tab-iss` before that click. A mouse click on desktop, or `touchscreen.tap` on iPad, is what the drive sends. The phone layout is unchanged. With the legend open, its box misses `#map-legend-toggle` and `#map-legend-panel`.
- `map-legend` starts closed. `#map-legend-toggle` is a left-edge `Legend` button, at least 44px, with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. A tap opens launch, day, twilight, and eclipse. A second tap, or Escape, closes it. The choice is not stored. On a short landscape screen the button sits to the right of `.maplibregl-ctrl-top-left` and both follow `env(safe-area-inset-left)`. With `body.shotlist-bar-visible` below 720px tall, `body.shotlist-bar-visible .view-map .map-legend` uses `left: calc(8px + env(safe-area-inset-left, 0px) + 52px + 8px)`, so the Legend stays clear of the compass. The iPhone 13 compass overlap of the Legend toggle is fixed by that offset. Below 520px the zoom stack starts 62px into the map. It does not name Anil's targets or Starship. Anil's targets stay indigo (`#8b93ff`) on the map, on cards, and in popups. Starship stays a red diamond when a public orbit is published.
- `map-imagery` is the imagery or clouds date. It lives in `#map-legend-panel` and is on screen only while that panel is open. Its box does not meet `#time-slider`. An IR line that says `feed unavailable` or `LIVE now (not the scrubbed time)` keeps that date hidden and shows a mark on the Legend button, described by `#map-legend-warning`.
- `map-controls` has no map ? button and no (i) credit button. The right tool rail uses that space. `#map-chrome-toggle` stays in the same screen place when it reads `Controls` and when it reads `Hide`. It is 88px wide and 44px tall, and `right` is `12px`.
- `map-time` is the Time strip (`.map-command`) laid on the map. The canvas meets `#map-pane` with a 0px gap. The strip's computed background is `rgba(16, 22, 28, 0.55)`, and the ground shows through it. It is not in the top Show toolbar. Below 520px tall the strip is a 52px row (`--map-command-height: 52px`). Its bottom is `--map-command-bottom`, `calc(var(--map-banner-clearance) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px))`, so the Now and skip buttons do not meet `#status-banner`. With a shoot list open on that short map, `main` padding-bottom is 0 and `--map-command-bottom` is `calc(4rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px))`. The strip sits on top of the bar. The right dock's max height subtracts `--map-command-bottom` and `--map-command-height`, so `#toggle-satellite-picker` scrolled to the end stays above the strip and Hide. The horizon inset bottom is `calc(var(--map-command-bottom) + var(--map-command-height) + 36px)`, so the preview stays above the lifted strip and Hide. A short landscape stays under the 800 by 600 gate. Keep the 52px row, that safe-area bottom, and the `4rem` lift together. T+45 moves the readout off Now, then Now returns it.
- `map-tools` toggles IR, night lights, labels, multi-orbit, and follow, and confirms ISS up is the selected bearing.
- `map-satellites` opens the picker and lists Tiangong and Hubble.
- `map-target-popup` opens the Verify Reef popup.
- `map-pin-drop` right-clicks the map and opens a pass popup.
- `map-hide-pin` hides Verify Reef from Queue and the targets source drops `verify-reef`.
- `map-launch` turns on Launches. The brief lists a shooting chance only. It names Verify Ascent and does not name Verify Horizon. The dialog opens from that name.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose the Map tab. The page also lands here on first load.
- The map opens clear. One `Controls` button sits at the bottom right. The legend tab, the Time strip on the map, the Show toolbar, the tool dock, the imagery date, the zoom buttons, the launch panel, and the satellite picker are hidden. The map ? button and the (i) credit button stay off. The top bar and the status footer stay, including Sign in and Reload when the session is dead. Help on the other tabs stays.
- Tap `Controls`. The chrome comes back and the button reads `Hide`. The legend is still a closed tab on the left. Tap `Legend` to open the rows and the imagery date. Tap `Hide` and the map is clear again. The chrome choice is stored on this device as `opd-map-chrome` (`shown` or `hidden`). A missing key stays hidden. The legend open or closed choice is not stored.

## Gotchas

- Follow starts on and recenters on the ISS. The script turns follow off, frames the pin at zoom 4, and clicks once. Clicks unwrap longitude toward that center before `project`. A fixture point across ±180 would otherwise land a world away, and `elementFromPoint` would miss the canvas.
- The map ? button and the (i) credit button are not on this tab. Queue, Upcoming, ISS view, Profile, and Log still have the corner ? button. The ISS view keeps its own credit control.
- IR replaces the daily clouds layer. Do not expect both buttons to stay active.
- Launch mode hides target pins. Drive the target popup before Launches.
- The launch pointer is valid for about 13 minutes. Each surface slides that clock forward before the page loads. Target coordinates stay the ones from `up`.
- Adding a satellite from the picker fetches CelesTrak. This drive only opens the list.
- Repo-root `targets.json` has eight `anils-targets` places, painted the same indigo on the map, on card chips, and in the target popup. The verify fixture categories are `coast` and `terrain`, so this drive checks the layer paint. It does not look for those eight names, and the legend has no swatch for them.
- A long press drops the same kind of pin as the right-click. The popup stays open through the click that follows the finger lift. `drive phone` is the touch proof. This drive right-clicks.
