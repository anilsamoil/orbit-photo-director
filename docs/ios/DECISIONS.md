Each row records the date, decision and reason. The D-series decisions are in [the design doc](../designs/ios-app.md).

| 2026-11-16 | The owner approved the design: a native shell first, native pieces later. | The approach and its premises were confirmed one by one during planning. |
| 2026-11-16 | Testing runs on GitHub's simulators and on a ground device through TestFlight or W-74 ad hoc delivery. | Physical-device tests cover background refresh, memory and notifications. NASA installs the App Store build in orbit. |
| 2026-11-16 | The route to orbit is an App Store listing, unlisted if Apple approves the request or public otherwise. | Both orbit devices need NASA approval, and NASA will not approve TestFlight for them. |
| 2026-11-16 | The orbit devices are managed and need NASA's approval for each app (gate G7); W-71 adds an approval packet. The project chooses public App Store distribution before submission and requests unlisted distribution. Apple fixes public versus private distribution at approval; switching between them then needs a new app record and review, while public-to-unlisted remains possible. Download size is not a constraint. | TestFlight is ground-only. Astra r2 and r3 on #373 and Apple's [distribution methods](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-your-apps-availability/set-distribution-methods/). |
| 2026-11-16 | Minimum OS is iOS and iPadOS 26.0. G1 and G2 use iPad and iPhone destinations on the closest available iOS 26 Simulator runtimes, plus the newest supported runtime when different. | 2026-11-16 | The app opens without a sign-in on a baseline of the curated targets and device-computed passes. The service-token fallback is dropped. | Owner request: different people use the app and set up their own profiles. App Review guidelines 4.8 and 5.1.1(v). |

| 2026-11-21 | Apple deletion uses a status receipt. Show an expected completion time within 24 hours of acceptance. Keep terminal status available for 30 days. | A user can confirm delayed completion after SNAP has removed the device credential. |

| 2026-11-21 | Use shared scored plans and personal-target sync for profiles outside the generator roster. Use coordinates and map pins for target entry on iOS. | Open enrollment has usable offline targets without a generator roster edit or an external geocoder. |
| 2026-11-21 | Freeze the known legacy browser inventory at cutover and publish the policy for unverified pre-cutover copies. | A site can verify server deletion and the migration of known contexts. An unreachable browser can still hold local copies. |

[Enrollment and recovery contract](ARCHITECTURE.md#signed-out-apple-deletion-recovery) |
| 2026-11-25 | D-13 sets the goal of an offline-first Earth viewer, like Google Earth with the ISS track on it, that works for hours to days without the Ku-band link. It adds the Earth pack (W-43, kept permanently), detail packs (W-45, zoom 9 to 12, 20 GB budget) and a globe view (W-44). The Earth pack is built from full-resolution NASA imagery and uses the Web Mercator output grid at zoom 6 to 8. At zoom 8, the grid is about 611 m per pixel at the equator. Only verified public targets may trigger target-derived tile requests. Personal targets use independently acquired imagery, such as region or bulk tiles selected without reference to a personal target. [Target request privacy](ARCHITECTURE.md#target-request-privacy) | Owner request. The merged plan's offline map stopped at zoom 5, about 5 km per pixel, plus a 512 MiB cache. |
