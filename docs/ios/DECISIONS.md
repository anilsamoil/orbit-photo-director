# Decisions

One line per decision made after the design doc: the date, the decision, the reason. Newest last. Decisions D-1 to D-10 are in [the design doc](../designs/ios-app.md) and are not repeated here.

| Date | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-11-16 | The owner approved the design: a native shell first, native pieces later. | The approach and its premises were confirmed one by one during planning. |
| 2026-11-16 | Testing runs in simulators on GitHub's Macs, plus one real device on Earth before anything goes to orbit. W-74 (ad hoc install) is a ground-only, unscheduled fallback, never an orbit route. | The owner could not get TestFlight onto the iPad in orbit. |
| 2026-11-16 | The route to orbit is the unlisted App Store listing. TestFlight serves the ground tester only. | The App Store on that iPad refuses TestFlight, while other App Store apps install. |
| 2026-11-16 | The orbit devices are managed and need NASA's approval for each app (gate G7); W-71 adds an approval packet. The project chooses public App Store distribution before submission and requests unlisted distribution. Apple fixes public versus private distribution at approval; switching between them then needs a new app record and review, while public-to-unlisted remains possible. Download size is not a constraint. | Owner report: TestFlight will not be approved; large downloads run overnight. Astra r2 and r3 on #373; Apple's [distribution methods](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-your-apps-availability/set-distribution-methods/). |
| 2026-11-16 | Minimum OS is iOS and iPadOS 26.0. Simulator runs for G1 and G2 use an iPadOS 26 runtime as close to 26.6 as the hosted runner offers, plus the newest runtime; the G3 ground device runs iOS or iPadOS 26. | The iPad in orbit is the 11-inch model on iPadOS 26.6.2; the iPhone runs iOS 26.6.1. |
| 2026-11-16 | The app opens without a sign-in on a baseline of the curated targets and device-computed passes. Each person signs in with Sign in with Apple or Google and gets their own profile; anyone with an Apple ID or Google account may sign up (owner decision, same day); the app offers sign-out and account deletion. The service-token fallback is dropped. | Owner request: different people use the app and set up their own profiles. App Review guidelines 4.8 and 5.1.1(v). |
| 2026-11-16 | After the independent spec review: the device queue starts from now, not from the end of the published horizon; added W-03 (signed host proof on a ground device), W-63 (old builds keep working), the update step in G2 and G3, month-independent log dedupe in W-20, and the orbit device details as an owner step. | 13 blocking review findings, recorded in the design doc. |
